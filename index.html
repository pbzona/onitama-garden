The Way of Onitama

Two masters, eight students, five cards. Each card is a martial form, and every form you use passes to your opponent.

  1. Your turn. Choose one of your two cards, then move one of your stones exactly as the card shows. The dark square is the stone; red squares are where it may go. Cards show moves from your own seat.
  2. Capture by landing on an opponent's stone. You may never land on your own.
  3. Pass the form on. The card you used slides to your opponent's side, and you take the waiting side card. You'll get it back only after your opponent uses it.
  4. No legal move? You must still give up one card and take the side card.

Two ways to win

石Way of the StoneCapture the opposing Master.
流Way of the StreamMove your Master onto your opponent's temple square, marked with the gold torii.

The seal on the dealt side card decides who opens: indigo 石 for Granite, vermilion 岩 for Basalt. Controls: click a card, then a stone, then a glowing square. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom, double-click empty space to recenter. Keys: V top-down view, C recenter, Z undo, H rules, M sound, Esc menu.
